Common questions

I bought PiMetric Pro. How do I get it back on a new or reinstalled iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID and open PiMetric. Pro usually unlocks by itself. If it doesn't, go to Settings → PiMetric Pro → Restore Purchases. You never pay twice.

How do I use a promo code?

In PiMetric, go to Settings → PiMetric Pro → Redeem a Code (iOS 16.3 or later), or open the link that came with your code. Apple checks the code; Pro unlocks right away.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. PiMetric Pro is a one-time purchase. There is nothing to cancel.

Where is my data? Will I lose my jobs if I delete the app?

Your calculations, jobs, material lists, formulas and photos are stored on your iPhone and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud (using Apple's CloudKit). We have no server and cannot see your data. If you delete the app, your data comes back from iCloud when you reinstall and open PiMetric. You can turn this off in PiMetric Settings → iCloud. To delete the copy in iCloud, open the iPhone Settings app › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › PiMetric › Delete Data.

How do I connect a Bluetooth laser?

Turn on the laser's Bluetooth, then in PiMetric go to Settings → Bluetooth Laser → Connect. Leica DISTO and Bosch GLM/PLR “C” models are supported (Pro).

Can I use PiMetric in Spanish?

Yes. It follows your iPhone's language, or pick Español in Settings → Language. Say It understands spoken Spanish too.

Are the results guaranteed?

PiMetric's results are estimates to help you plan. Always confirm against your local building codes, a qualified professional, or official aviation sources. Health and pregnancy tools are not medical advice, and the paycheck, retirement and other money tools are not tax or financial advice.
