PiMetric Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

PiMetric is a calculator app. We built it so your work stays on your iPhone. We do not run accounts, we do not collect personal information, and we do not track you.

What stays on your device

Your calculations, saved answers, Field Log entries, jobs, material lists and prices, your own saved formulas, notes, settings and any photos you attach are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Camera and photos

If you choose to attach a photo to a calculation, PiMetric asks for access to your camera or photo library. Photos you attach are kept on your device and are only shared when you export or share them yourself.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you use "Say It" to speak an equation, PiMetric asks for microphone and speech recognition access. Speech is turned into text on your device when your iPhone supports it; otherwise Apple's speech recognition service processes it under Apple's privacy policy. PiMetric does not record or store your audio.

Bluetooth laser meters

If you connect a laser distance meter, PiMetric asks for Bluetooth access and talks only to that meter to receive measurements. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Siri, widget and Apple Watch

To show your latest answer and current job on the Home Screen widget and the Apple Watch app, PiMetric keeps a small summary in storage shared only between its own app, widget and Watch app on your devices. Siri shortcuts just open PiMetric; what you say to Siri is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Currency exchange rates

The Currency tool downloads public exchange rates from the Frankfurter service (api.frankfurter.dev). That request contains no information about you or your calculations; like any web request it reveals your device's IP address to that service.

Exports and sharing

When you export a PDF or CSV, the file is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you.

PiMetric Pro purchases

PiMetric Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple ID. The app asks the App Store whether Pro is owned so it can unlock it on your devices. Promo (offer) codes are entered on Apple's own screen and checked by Apple.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

PiMetric contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Children

PiMetric does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
