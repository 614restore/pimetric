Exchange rates

The Currency tool shows public reference rates from the European Central Bank (through the Frankfurter service) and from ExchangeRate-API. They are updated about once a day, are for information only, and may differ from what a bank, card network or exchange office charges. They are not suitable for trading. If the rate services are unavailable, PiMetric shows the last rates it saved, with their date.
